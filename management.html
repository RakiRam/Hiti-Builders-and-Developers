K Manideep Reddy

Founder & CEO

The company was promoted by Sri K Manideep Reddy, Civil Engineer with Masters in Structural Engineering from JNTU Hyderabad, who is renowned for his Technical acumen & Management excellence in Engineering & Construction Industry. With over 10+ years of experience in Engineering, business leadership, strategic planning, detailed engineering and project management, he has worked on a number of projects in Power Generation, Power Transmission, Telecom Infrastructure, Heavy Industrial structures, commercial and residential projects in India and Overseas. Beyond that Company had also constructed several residential and commercial buildings in Hyderabad. Reddy believe that one must always address the best interest of the client — a vital factor for the construction business. He says, We found people looking for Builders who can make their dream home with high quality and high standards for reasonable cost. Then we started delivering their dream home.
